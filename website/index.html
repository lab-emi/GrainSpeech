02 / The idea

Less context.
More acoustic detail.

GrainSpeech explores how encoder context and Mel supervision affect compact speech synthesis. Its acoustic model has 264.8K parameters.

01

Focus the encoder

A fixed-receptive-field convolutional encoder uses local phoneme context to predict pitch, energy and duration.

02

Preserve fine structure

L1, SSIM and a Mel-specific gradient-variance objective supervise local detail along time and frequency.
